# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hyprland desktop users spanning two ends of the same spectrum: power users who want a capable, keyboard-adjacent shell, and ricing enthusiasts who want a setup that looks impressive in screenshots and in daily use. Both groups demand quality — they notice corners cut. They run this fulltime, not just for demos. Context: multi-monitor Linux desktops, dark environments, constant ambient visibility.

## Product Purpose

ActivSpot is a Dynamic Island shell for Hyprland built with Quickshell/QML. It acts as a hybrid between a HUD and a shell — ambient status display that requires zero attention most of the time, but expands into a capable action surface on demand. Music, clock, weather, notifications, timer, file stash, volume, Discord status, and a configurable topbar, all in a single floating island at the top of the screen. Success means it disappears into the workflow while still being the most polished thing on the desktop.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, functional, elegant. High craft without showmanship — the polish is felt, not announced. It earns attention through quality, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic Waybar: flat, utilitarian, no personality, zero presence
- KDE Plasma-style widgets: heavy, nested, visually cluttered
- macOS recreation: derivative, no original design identity
- Neon anime rice: glowing primaries, maximalist color noise, r/unixporn excess

## Design Principles

1. **HUD first, shell second.** Default state is ambient and unobtrusive. Expansions are deliberate, never accidental. Every pixel of resting state earns its keep.
2. **No decoration that doesn't inform.** Motion, color, and surface effects must carry meaning — state change, focus, hierarchy. Decorative animation is waste.
3. **Adaptive identity.** The shell reacts to context: music playing, weather shifting, game running, Discord active. It feels aware of the desktop, not bolted onto it.
4. **Precision over prettiness.** Spacing, sizing, and timing are exact. A slightly-off radius or misaligned text reads as unfinished. Craft is the standard, not the goal.
5. **Theme-agnostic structure.** Layout and hierarchy must work across all six themes (Mocha, Nord, Apple, Carbon, Midnight, Matugen). Color is a layer; structure is the foundation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Reasonable contrast on all text is expected, particularly on glass surfaces where background blur reduces effective contrast. No reduced-motion requirement, but animation timings are user-configurable in settings.
